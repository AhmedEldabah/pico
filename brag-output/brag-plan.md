# Brag Plan: PICO Modern Agriculture

## What is this app?
A bilingual (EN/AR + RTL), multi-page marketing site for PICO — a third-generation Egyptian fruit grower and exporter — with a live "place an order" app hero, real photogrammetry 3D fruit you can spin, and a full seasonal crop calendar.

## The angle
A premium heritage-brand harvest film. Not a startup gag — this is a real 50-year-old grower, so the flex is craft + scale: family since 1974, Egypt's #4 strawberry producer, 75% of Egypt's grape exports, and a site that lets you literally spin their fruit and order it. Quiet confidence, crimson brand, real product.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The PICO wordmark strikes in over cream, the green leaf drops onto it, and the line "As fresh as it gets." settles. One clean, premium hit — the brand, immediately.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real 3D strawberry rotating in space (their actual photogrammetry model), then snapping to mango, then citrus.
- The live app: fresh-produce feed, a tap on "+", and the toast "Order placed — harvesting now."
- The colorful crop calendar bars sweeping in — the whole harvest, month by month.
- Two credibility stats counting/snapping in: "Since 1974", "#4 strawberry producer worldwide".

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup on crimson, "As fresh as it gets. From our farms straight to your home." then the wordmark. Warm, confident, done.

## User flow worth showing
The PICO app hero: browse the fresh-produce feed → tap add on a product → "Order placed — harvesting now." That's the entry → action → result. Secondary flow: spin a 3D fruit and switch varieties.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (with polished restraint)
- Creative direction: a quiet, premium harvest film for a heritage fruit brand
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, big type, confident motion; crimson + cream + one leaf-green accent; no jokey energy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: target ~20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #fffdfc (cream white); soft-pink sections #fddce1
- Accent: #d60633 (PICO crimson); gradient #c4052e → #ea6288
- Leaf accent: #7ac143
- Text: #212529
- Display font: Montserrat (800)
- Body font: Montserrat (500/600)
- Strongest visual element: the real 3D fruit + the crimson brand + the animated order app + the colorful crop calendar

## Share copy (draft)
As fresh as it gets. 🍓 A 50-year-old Egyptian fruit house, rebuilt: spin their real 3D fruit, order from a live app, and read the whole harvest off one crop calendar.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, elegant upbeat bed with a confident lift; motion-matched accents.
- Music: bundled happy-beats business track (upbeat but clean); start at 0, gentle fade-in, fade out under the outro.
- Music treatment: hold volume moderate so type reads; small swell into the calendar sweep and the outro lockup.
- Music cue guidance: use the bundled track's cue preset if present; target strong cues at the logo hit (~0.6s), the app toast (~10s), and the outro lockup (~17s). Sequential calendar bars land on a beat-grid window but hold the full set after.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — a soft glow pulse on the crimson lockup only.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched — whoosh on scene seams, a soft tick on the stat count, a light click on the app "+" tap and a positive chime on the "Order placed" toast.
- Audio-coupled moments: app "+" tap + toast, stat count-up, calendar bars arriving.
- Restraint rule: no busy UI clicks everywhere, no waveform bars; sound supports, never decorates.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Logo strike (hook) — 3s
Cream frame. The PICO wordmark scales in fast and settles; the green leaf drops onto the "i"; "As fresh as it gets." fades up beneath. Hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes — leaf drops after the wordmark settles.
Audio intent: one clean premium hit; music starts.
Audio-coupled idea: soft impact on the wordmark settle, tiny tick on the leaf drop.
Music: warm elegant lift begins.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The 3D fruit — 5s
Soft-pink ground. The real 3D strawberry rotates in and turns; captions "Real fruit. Spin it." small. It snaps to mango, then citrus (quick variety swaps).
Sequential/interaction: yes — strawberry → mango → citrus swap on beats.
Audio intent: gentle wonder; a light whoosh per swap.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle whoosh on each fruit swap.
Music: steady bed.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The order app — 5s
The PICO phone, screen upright. The produce feed scrolls; a product row highlights; a cursor taps "+"; the toast "Order placed — harvesting now." rises.
Sequential/interaction: yes — feed scroll → tap "+" → toast.
Audio intent: satisfying, tactile.
Audio-coupled idea: light click on the tap, positive chime on the toast.
Music: bed with a small lift into the toast.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Heritage stats — 3.5s
Crimson field. Two big lines snap in and hold: "Since 1974" then "#4 strawberry producer, worldwide." Small "75% of Egypt's grape exports" beneath.
Sequential/interaction: yes — stat lines arrive one by one, each held to read.
Audio intent: confident, weighty.
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick as each stat lands.
Music: swell begins.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Crop calendar + lockup (outro) — 4s
The colorful crop-calendar bars sweep in left-to-right (strawberry red, grape purple, mango gold, avocado green…). They settle, then the frame resolves to the crimson PICO lockup: wordmark + "As fresh as it gets. From our farms straight to your home."
Sequential/interaction: yes — bars sweep in on the beat, then hold.
Audio intent: warm resolve; music peaks then fades.
Audio-coupled idea: light sweep as bars fill; soft glow pulse on the lockup.
Music: peak then fade out.
Transition mood: soft fade to end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but elegant / cinematic-lite
**Audio summary:** a warm, confident bed that opens on the logo hit, stays out of the way while the fruit and app do the talking, and swells gently into the crop-calendar sweep and the final crimson lockup before fading.
